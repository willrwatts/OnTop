Overview

What We Provide

With the help of our generous donators and volunteers, we are able to provide dynamic online resources as well as social events which help spread awareness of our presence and the resources we provide. We have volunteers ranging widely in age, race, identity, and region and are happy to accept help from anybody of any background. We have partnered with many companies, including medical resources, which allow us to create our online practices.

Community Work

Our main outreach is through social events such as conventions and community events. These events serve as a way for us to reach out to communities which may not have easy access to our online resources. Each year we dedicate a portion of our resources towards providing these communities with the ability to access our online resources.
