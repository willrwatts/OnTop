Reach Out

We are always here in your time of need. Talk to our professional counselors who are available 24/7 to listen and help you take the next step forward.

Reach Out Now

Here at OnTop, we are dedicated to serving people of all backgrounds and identities. If you are struggling with mental health, especially ADHD, anxiety, depression, and/or OCD, we highly recommend our free online resources and assistance. Our professional counselors are on call 24/7 to listen and guide

Virtual Support Groups

OCD and Daily Life

Hear from other people with OCD about how to cope with the stress of OCD in your day-to-day life.

LGBTQ+ Support

A safe place for anyone to voice their concerns and help others with similar worries. Whether you yourself are LGBTQ+ or someone you know is, you are welcomed to join!

Art Therapy Group

Utilizing art as a means of treating depression, anxiety, OCD, and ADHD. Anyone is welcome to join. We offer both online meetings and in-person meetings at our OnTop locations.

Mindfulness Based Stress Reduction

Helping develop mindfulness practices in order to reduce the stress of school, work, or day-to-day life. Can help improve focus, sleep, and time management.

Co-Ed Process Group

Exploring themes such as identity, self-esteem, body image, and mental health.

Dealing with Depression

Learning with others how to cope with depression and continue your daily life with it.
