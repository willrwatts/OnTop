Resources

Our online library of resources to help assist with short-term and long-term issues.

Find Resources

Calming Practices

A gallery of our best practices for grounding and slowing down. Practices can be used regarding short-term or long-term problems, however, these practices are not "cures" or solutions to the problem, they only help calm temporarily. Most practices work best in a quiet and familiar environment.

Progressive Muscle Relaxation

Starting from your feet and moving towards your head, this practice aims to relax each part of you body individually before focusing on the greater whole.

Guided Imagery

Follow a video which helps you visualize and evoke mental images and senses. This includes touch, sight, sounds, tastes, and smells.

5 Minute Breathing Exercise

A simple, video directed, breathing exercise to help and relax the senses. Great for those with racing thoughts and tunnel vision.

Tai Chi Exercise

Tai Chi is an ancient Chinese martial art which was initially developed for self defence. Over time, it has evolved into a low effort, gentile, and meditative exercise.

Calm Music Meditation

Video segments ranging from 5 minutes to 30 minutes. Each with meditative music to meditate to or go about your day-to-day life with.

Guided Art Exercise

A 10 minute video exercise which utilizing simple drawings to calm and slow down the brain. A writing tool and paper is required.

Resource Gallery

External resources provided by OnTop for you to utilize. These organizations are equally dedicated to help you as we are and are also available 24/7.

National Institute of Mental Health

A national organization leading federal efforts to promote prevention, treatment, and recovery supports for Americans with mental health conditions.

Mental Health First Aid

A course that teaches you how to identify, understand and respond to signs of mental illnesses and substance use disorders. The training gives you the skills you need to reach out and provide initial help and support to someone.

Mental Health America

An organization who is advancing the mental health and well-being of all people living in the U.S. through public education, research, advocacy and public policy, and direct service.

Anxiety and Depression Association of America

Raising awareness, improving diagnosis and treatment, and ultimately finding a cure for anxiety, depression, OCD, PTSD, and related disorders through education, research, and clinical practice.

S.A.F.E. Alternatives

Providing outpatient services to adolescents and adults who engage in self-destructive, impulsive behaviors and have a desire to stop.

The Trevor Project

The leading suicide prevention and crisis intervention nonprofit organization for LGBTQ+ young people.

App

We have partnered with Seattle Design Studio to create an app which provides services 24/7 and updates users on new services and practices.
