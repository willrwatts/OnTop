Who Are We

View our goals, beliefs, and mission. Get a better understanding of who we are and why we are trying to help.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help teenagers and young adults understand and accept their stress and anxiety through simple, yet effective methods that anyone can do. We specialize in helping those with ADHD, depression, generalized anxiety, and OCD.

Accessibility

We believe that our services should not be exclusive or hard to recieve. Here at OnTop, we work to make our services as accessible as possible. This includes providing free services as well working with healthcare providers to get the best deals for those we support. Although most of our presence is online, we try our best to host and sponsor events which spread our reach towards smaller and more isolated communities.

Impartiality

In order to provide the best care for the world, we do not discriminate or take sides in any conflict. We care for all people the same and do not see any race, gender, sexuality, identity, or views as lesser than another.

Humanity

We believe that our services are one step forward in a larger push towards understanding and normalizing mental health among young adults and teens. We always prioritize our patients concerns and feelings first and see each other as humans rather than numbers or statitstics.
